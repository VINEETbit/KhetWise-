# LinkedIn caption

🌱 Introducing **KhetWise** — a smart agriculture platform I’ve been building.

My goal is to bring useful farm information and practical digital tools together in one place, so farmers can move from a question to a clearer next step.

KhetWise brings together:

• Crop and fertilizer recommendations based on growing conditions  
• Yield and market price estimates  
• Location-based weather forecasts and a regional soil guide  
• An English and Hindi guided Farm Assistant with voice input  
• A dashboard for farm details and agricultural tools

Behind the dashboard, a React and Vite frontend connects to an Express and MongoDB backend, a FastAPI prediction service, and weather data from Open-Meteo. The carousel shows the platform architecture and a farmer’s path through it.

I’m building KhetWise as a decision-support tool: predictions are estimates, and the assistant provides general guidance that should be checked against local conditions and expert advice.

This project has been a great way to bring web development, machine learning and agriculture together. I’d love to hear feedback from people working in farming and agri-tech.
